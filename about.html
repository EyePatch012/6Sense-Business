About Us

We work with what most business methods overlook: the hidden dynamics, energetic blocks, and systemic patterns that silently shape your performance.

We are a leadership and culture transformation team guiding enterprise reinvention through the 6th Sense Method – a methodology rooted in systemic intelligence, somatic wisdom, and intuitive knowing. With passion and integrity, we dedicate ourselves to creating meaningful change.

Through our business constellations and leadership programs, we invite organizations and leaders to explore new perspectives and take responsibility for shaping a healthier world.

We don’t just develop leaders, we awaken them – to themselves, to their organizations, and to the wider ecosystems they influence. In our experience working with personal and organizational transformation, most change initiatives lead only to temporary improvements. Why? Because the deeper, often unconscious dynamics remain untouched. These underlying patterns—of behavior, thinking, and context—drain energy and reduce an organization’s ability to truly adapt.

Hidden Dynamics = Drained Energy = Lost Momentum = Missed Potential

Releasing Hidden Dynamics = Renewed Energy = Natural Flow = Sustainable Growth

Our approach is grounded in the understanding that organizations, like living systems, thrive when they are in balance. When hidden dynamics are revealed, energy is released and leadership capacity expands. This not only fuels healthier teams and stronger cultures, but also creates ripple effects that reach beyond the organization itself. By cultivating awareness of the larger systems we are part of, leaders begin to act from a place of responsibility and renewal. In this way, transformation inside organizations naturally contributes to resilience, continuity, and a more sustainable future for the environments and communities around them. At the core of every thriving business lies one invisible force: momentum. It's the flow where everything aligns — ideas, decisions, opportunities, collaboration, and profit.

When an organization is in momentum, things move with ease. Progress feels natural. Growth becomes exponential. At 6Sense Business Constellations, we uncover the hidden energy leaks within your organizational system. We bring to light the blocks that are holding your team back and guide you back to a state of momentum: alignment, clarity, and forward movement.

Through Business Constellations and the 6th Sense method, we approach organizations as living human systems. This method helps leaders uncover hidden entanglements—repeating patterns that trap teams in inefficiency and block the healthy flow of collective energy. These entanglements are rarely addressed through conventional strategies, yet they are often the root cause of resistance to change.

When these systemic dynamics are revealed, leaders gain access to a broader field of insight. This expanded awareness allows them to navigate tensions in a way that directly addresses and resolves them — strengthening the business and creating forward momentum. Because real change doesn’t happen by addressing symptoms — it requires seeing the whole and shifting from the source. The result: An organization that gets back in sync — with empowered decisions, renewed collaboration, and a strong sense of direction. And most importantly: a return to sustainable momentum that’s felt across the culture and reflected in the bottom line.
